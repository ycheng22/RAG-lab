# Token Hotels & Resorts — Dining Guide

Every Token Hotels & Resorts property has at least one restaurant run by a chef from the region. Menus change with the seasons, and hotel guests can book any restaurant through the front desk or the app.

## Token Lisbon

### Maré — Token Lisbon

- **Cuisine:** Portuguese seafood
- **Chef:** Inês Carvalho
- **Hours:** 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM, closed Sundays
- **Dress code:** Smart casual
- **Reservations:** Recommended

Chef Inês Carvalho grew up in a fishing family in Peniche and buys her fish at the Cascais market every morning. The signature dish at Maré is the cataplana of clams and chorizo.
At Maré the tasting menu costs $95 per person, drinks not included.

## Token Kyoto

### Hanare — Token Kyoto

- **Cuisine:** Kaiseki
- **Chef:** Kenji Morimoto
- **Hours:** 6:00 PM - 9:30 PM
- **Dress code:** Smart; no shorts or sandals
- **Reservations:** Required at least 3 days ahead

Chef Kenji Morimoto trained for twelve years in Gion before opening Hanare, which seats only fourteen guests. The signature dish at Hanare is the seasonal hassun platter.
At Hanare the tasting menu costs $210 per person, drinks not included.

## Token Vancouver

### Tidewater Grill — Token Vancouver

- **Cuisine:** Pacific Northwest
- **Chef:** Maya Fontaine
- **Hours:** 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM
- **Dress code:** Casual
- **Reservations:** Recommended on weekends

Chef Maya Fontaine works with First Nations fishers and local foragers, and the menu changes every month. The signature dish at Tidewater Grill is the cedar-plank salmon.
At Tidewater Grill the tasting menu costs $120 per person, drinks not included.

## Token Queenstown

### Ridgeline — Token Queenstown

- **Cuisine:** Modern New Zealand
- **Chef:** Tom Hargreaves
- **Hours:** 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM
- **Dress code:** Casual
- **Reservations:** Walk-ins welcome

Chef Tom Hargreaves sources lamb from a high-country station forty minutes from the hotel. The signature dish at Ridgeline is the slow-roasted lamb shoulder.
Ridgeline serves à la carte only; there is no tasting menu.

## Token Marrakech

### Dar Zitoun — Token Marrakech

- **Cuisine:** Moroccan
- **Chef:** Samira Benali
- **Hours:** 7:30 PM - 11:30 PM
- **Dress code:** Smart casual
- **Reservations:** Required

Chef Samira Benali cooks her grandmother's recipes over charcoal, and the bread is baked in the riad's own oven. The signature dish at Dar Zitoun is the seven-hour lamb tagine with preserved lemon.
At Dar Zitoun the tasting menu costs $85 per person, drinks not included.

## Token Singapore

### Lantern Room — Token Singapore

- **Cuisine:** Modern Peranakan
- **Chef:** Daniel Lim
- **Hours:** 6:00 PM - 10:30 PM
- **Dress code:** Smart elegant
- **Reservations:** Required

Chef Daniel Lim reworks Nyonya family dishes with modern technique, and the tasting menu has nine courses. The signature dish at Lantern Room is the lobster laksa.
At Lantern Room the tasting menu costs $240 per person, drinks not included.

### Skyline Bar — Token Singapore

- **Cuisine:** Rooftop cocktails and small plates
- **Chef:** Head bartender Priya Nair
- **Hours:** 5:00 PM - 1:00 AM
- **Dress code:** Smart casual
- **Reservations:** Not accepted

Skyline Bar sits beside the infinity pool and is first come, first served. The signature dish at Skyline Bar is the the Marina Sling cocktail.
Skyline Bar serves à la carte only; there is no tasting menu.

## Token Cusco

### Inti Kitchen — Token Cusco

- **Cuisine:** Andean
- **Chef:** Rosa Quispe
- **Hours:** 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM and 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM
- **Dress code:** Casual
- **Reservations:** Walk-ins welcome

Chef Rosa Quispe grows potatoes and quinoa with her family in the Sacred Valley. The signature dish at Inti Kitchen is the alpaca loin with red quinoa.
Inti Kitchen serves à la carte only; there is no tasting menu.
