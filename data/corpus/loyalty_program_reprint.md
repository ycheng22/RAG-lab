# Token Circle — the Token Hotels & Resorts loyalty program

_Reprinted from the guest welcome booklet._

Token Circle costs nothing to join. Members earn points on every stay and unlock more benefits as they stay more nights in a calendar year.

## Earning and using points

Members earn 10 points for every dollar spent on rooms, dining and spa treatments. 10,000 points can be exchanged for one free night in a Standard room at any property. Points expire after 18 months without any account activity.

## Membership tiers

### Member

Every member starts at the Member tier. Benefits: faster premium Wi-Fi; member rates 5% below public rates.

### Silver

Reach Silver after 10 nights in a calendar year. Benefits: 10% off dining; late checkout until 1 PM.

### Gold

Reach Gold after 25 nights in a calendar year. Benefits: room upgrade when available; late checkout until 2 PM; free breakfast.

### Platinum

Reach Platinum after 50 nights in a calendar year. Benefits: guaranteed late checkout until 4 PM; lounge access; one free night every year.
