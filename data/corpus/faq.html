Token Hotels & Resorts — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our front desks hear most. For anything else, contact the hotel directly.

Reservations and check-in

Can I check in early? Early check-in depends on availability on the day. To guarantee a room from 9:00 AM, book the Early Arrival add-on for $50.

Can I check out late? Late checkout until 2:00 PM costs $40 and must be requested at least one day before departure. Token Circle Silver, Gold and Platinum members receive late checkout as a benefit.

Do you charge a deposit? A refundable incidentals hold of $100 per night is placed on your card at check-in and released at checkout.

Wi-Fi and connectivity

Is Wi-Fi free? Yes. Wi-Fi is free for every guest at every Token Hotels & Resorts property. The network name and password are printed on your key card sleeve.

Pets

Can I bring my dog? Dogs under 10 kg are welcome at Token Lisbon, Token Vancouver and Token Queenstown for a fee of $30 per night. Other properties accept registered assistance animals only.

Parking

Is there parking? Token Vancouver and Token Singapore offer valet parking for $45 per night. Parking is free at Token Queenstown and Token Cusco. Token Lisbon partners with a public garage next door at $25 per day. Token Kyoto, Token Marrakech and Token Reykjavík have no parking.

Accessibility

Do you have accessible rooms? Every property has step-free accessible rooms except Token Cusco, whose colonial building has no elevator. Token Cusco does have two ground-floor rooms with wide doors; please call ahead.

Children

Do children stay free? Children under 12 stay free when they share a room with their parents and use existing bedding. Cots are free on request.

Payments

Which cards do you accept? We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express at every property. Cash is accepted in the local currency.
