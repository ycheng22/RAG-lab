# Token Hotels & Resorts — Cancellation Policy (2025)

Effective 1 March 2025. This policy replaces the 2024 cancellation policy for all bookings made on or after that date.

## Flexible rate

Guests on the Flexible rate may cancel free of charge up to 72 hours before the arrival date. Cancellations are made online, in the app, or by calling the hotel.

## Advance Saver rate

The Advance Saver rate is 15% cheaper than the Flexible rate, is paid in full at booking, and is non-refundable. Advance Saver bookings cannot be changed or cancelled.

## Late cancellation and no-shows

If you cancel a Flexible booking less than 72 hours before arrival, we charge the first night's rate. If you do not arrive and do not cancel, the no-show fee is the full stay.

## Group bookings

Bookings of 5 or more rooms can be cancelled free of charge up to 14 days before arrival.
