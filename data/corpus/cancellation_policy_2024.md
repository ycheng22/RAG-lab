# Token Hotels & Resorts — Cancellation Policy (2024)

Effective 1 January 2024. This policy applies to all bookings made directly with Token Hotels & Resorts.

## Free cancellation

Guests may cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the arrival date. Cancellations are made online, in the app, or by calling the hotel.

## Late cancellation and no-shows

If you cancel less than 48 hours before arrival, we charge the first night's rate. If you do not arrive and do not cancel, the no-show fee is the first night's rate.

## Changes to a booking

You can change the dates of a booking free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival, subject to availability. The new rate may be higher or lower than the original one.
